# Le doute devient une vente

10 s, 1920×1080, 60 FPS, 600 frames, MP4 H.264. Cinematic 3D desk with mechanical typewriter, 1990s desk trading phone and generic paper props, transitioning into a 3D Japanese candlestick chart with red and green bodies and wicks and animated sell arrows. Final title: **LE DOUTE DEVIENT UNE VENTE**.

**Historical integrity:** Editorial text is explicitly marked as such, not an authentic newspaper headline or quotation. Generic paper props are not genuine archival photographs. Candlesticks are illustrative synthetic shapes, **not** actual GBP historical quotes, times, or trading volumes. The trading desk is a stylized 3D reconstruction, not photorealistic footage. No invented price or quote is displayed.

`bun i && bun run build && bun run render` creates `out/DoubtBecomesSelling1992.mp4`.
